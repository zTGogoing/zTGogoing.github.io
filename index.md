## 👀 About Me
I am currently a Master's student in Electronic Information at the **Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)**. Prior to that, I received my B.E. degree in Software Engineering from **Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA)** in July 2023.

My research focuses on **Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI)**, **Neural Signal Processing**, and **Deep Learning for Time-Series Data**. I am particularly interested in closed-loop BCI systems for motor rehabilitation, domain-generalization EEG decoding algorithms, and dynamic brain network reorganization under neural stimulation.
